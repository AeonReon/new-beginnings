# 03 · Isolation is the pitfall

**The thing that ends families' attempts is not curriculum and it is not
academics. It is isolation.**

No community. No friends for the children. No other parents to hand a hard week
to. A parent alone at a kitchen table, being teacher, cook and entertainment for
eleven hours a day, is the picture most people carry of taking their children's
upbringing back — and it is not an unfair picture. It is exactly the part these
projects exist to fix.

## Why people default to the institution

Two honest reasons, and pretending otherwise makes us useless:

1. **It supplies the social life.** Friends, a peer group, a hall full of other
   children, without the parent having to build any of it.
2. **It is free daycare.** A parent has to earn. For most families that is not
   an ideological position, it is arithmetic.

Any alternative that does not solve both of those is a hobby, not an option.

## So the answer has to include all of it

- **Community first** — other families, other parents, friends for the children.
- The best of every tradition and method, taken on merit rather than loyalty.
- The best learning tools available now.
- Fun, and hours outdoors.
- **Somewhere for parents to work while the children play** — so nobody has to
  choose between providing and being present.

Children who love their life, parents present and still earning, and a group of
families around both. That is the target.

## Where it shows

The "Be part of it" block at the foot of the Conscious Parenting home page states
