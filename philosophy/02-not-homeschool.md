# 02 · This is not a home-schooling app

Calling it one gets it wrong, and the mistake is expensive: it halves the
audience on the first sentence and it makes an enemy of every parent whose child
is in school.

**It is for people who want to take responsibility for their own children** —
because it is the highest-value thing they can do, and because being deeply
involved at every stage is what builds the bond. The point is that a child grows
up certain, with no doubt in it, that their parents were there and their parents
cared.

Whether the child is in school, at home, or somewhere in between is a **separate
question, and the app stays neutral on it.** A parent whose child is in school
full-time is exactly as welcome and gets exactly as much use out of everything
here.

## The rule that follows, and it is not negotiable

**No copy anywhere may read as a verdict on another family's choice.**

That includes copy that is true. "The school day was built around a working day
that no longer exists" is defensible and it belongs in a drop-down for somebody
who went looking for it. It does not belong in a headline, because a mother
reading it on her phone at the school gate hears an accusation and closes the
app, and she was exactly who we were built for.

## Practical test

Read any new headline as if you are a parent who works full-time, whose child is
in school, and who is doing their best. If it stings, rewrite it. The argument
against the default can be made — it just has to be made to somebody who has
already chosen to listen, not shouted at somebody walking past.

See `04-excellence.md` for the positive version, which is the one that leads.
