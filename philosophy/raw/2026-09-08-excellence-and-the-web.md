# 2026-09-08 — Excellence, not struggle. And the web of everything.

_Verbatim. Said while reworking the Light School page. Lightly punctuated for
readability; no words changed, added or removed._

> In the Light School, when you click into that section, I really don't like the
> card and the description at all. It's doing a sales pitch against traditional
> education, but a lot of people there already have that idea. It's more — we
> need to sell what we are doing, and then the whole app can challenge the idea
> of traditional education. So it needs to be shouting about our benefit.
>
> Even the layout — I don't like the way it's just too text heavy. It's not easy
> for the eyes to read. I think it needs different things going on, and it needs
> to start with the real premise, that is about people who want to take their
> children's future into their own hands, because no one cares more than the
> parent — or at least no one should care more than the parent — because that is
> basically the most important thing in our life: to raise our children well and
> give them the best advantages we can.
>
> We have lots of online apps and resources, as well as local meet-ups, and the
> full web of everything you need to excel at parenting.
>
> I don't know whether to put this or not, but it seems all the other parenting
> groups — and for example Parenting NI — it's all about the struggle.
> Everything's about funding for problems and damage and stress and worry,
> whereas we want to focus the other way. We are focused on excellence, becoming
> the best we can be, thriving — the complete opposite of what all the others do.
> We want to take your child and give them everything possible to absolutely
> excel and charge ahead, without being held behind because everyone's focused on
> those who are struggling. We recognise that the great in society are what push
> it forward, and we want to develop them.
>
> I want that message exciting. It matches all what we're doing with Days Out. It
> matches Happy Weather. Everything is focusing on the win, on the exciting,
> taking wherever we begin from and pushing forward and being the best we can and
> finding every resource we can to do it, and making it look graceful along the
> way.
>
> All of this is being refined as we go, it's evolving in real time, and I think
> it's starting to come together and be exciting now that we see the apps. I
> don't know if anyone else in any parenting field is doing this level of support
> and structure, from everything — the apps we have.
