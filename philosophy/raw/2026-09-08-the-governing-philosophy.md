# 2026-09-08 — The governing philosophy: not homeschool, and the isolation pitfall

_Verbatim, from earlier the same day. Lightly punctuated; no words changed._

> We'll need to start thinking about the philosophy a bit more of the whole app,
> because it's definitely not homeschool. It's just for people who want to take
> responsibility for their own children, because you believe it's the highest
> value thing you can do in your life is to raise great children, and to be
> deeply involved in every stage of the process to build that bond with them, and
> let them know that their parents were always there and their parents always
> cared.
>
> And then the goal of this app and project and community is so that we don't hit
> the number one pitfall that many would hit, and that would be ending up
> isolated — without a community, without friends for the children, without other
> parents. Because that's why people default to government institutions: it's
> because of the social aspect, and it also becomes a free daycare.
>
> So if we can take the best of everything — the best of every tradition, the best
> of every method — we have community, we have the best learning tools, we have
> fun, we have outdoors, we have children who love their life, and a place for
> parents to work, then that's the ultimate path to winning.
