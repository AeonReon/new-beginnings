# 05 · The web of everything

The offer is not an app. It is **the full web of everything you need to excel at
parenting**, and the fact that it already exists and is already free is the part
nobody believes until they look.

## What is in it today

- **The online school** — Light School, for adults and children, anywhere. An
  action, a skill and a piece of knowledge a day, tailored to where you are.
- **Local meet-ups** — families on the North Coast and in Mid Antrim, out
  together every week. Beaches, forests, farms, rock pools, a coach hired
  between us.
- **The apps** — Days Out NI, Happy Weather, Fuel Price NI, Mo & Pip, Conscious
  Parenting NI. All free, all built here, and they work together.
- **The resources** — the best of every tradition, gathered and tested, so a
  parent chooses from good options instead of searching.
- **A path that adapts** — six strands, five rungs, shaped by facilitators, AI
  and the parent together.
- **The other parents** — the part that decides whether any of the rest lasts.

## Why the web is the product

Any one of these is a nice free thing. Together they are a claim: that a family
in Northern Ireland can have days out, weather that tells them when to go, the
cheapest way to get there, letters for the small one, the reasoning behind every
practice, and a group of families to do it with — for nothing, from one place.

The open question the user keeps returning to, and it is worth answering
honestly: **is anyone else in the parenting field doing this level of support and
structure across this many pieces?** Not that we have found. That is either an
enormous opportunity or a sign that it is hard to sustain, and probably both.

## The pack

`APPS/ni-apps` (ni-apps.vercel.app) is the hand-out page — every app, with
install instructions for iPhone, Android and Mac. It currently lists four; Mo &
Pip is the missing one. The next step is turning it from *a list of apps* into
*a pack*: one reason to take all of it, one QR that hands the lot over.

## Order of operations

Community first, always. A parent who has other parents will forgive a missing
feature. A parent with a perfect app and nobody to talk to leaves.
