# 01 · The premise

**Nobody cares more about your child than you do — or at least nobody should.**

That single sentence is the foundation. Everything else is worked out from it.

Raising a child well is the most important work of a life. Not a phase of it, not
a thing done alongside the real work — the thing itself. If that is true, then
the person with the most at stake is the parent, and no teacher, assistant or
institution, however good and however well-meaning, has more interest in how that
child turns out.

From that follows the whole posture of these projects: **take your children's
future into your own hands, and give them every advantage you can while you do
it.** Not because anyone else is bad at their job, but because responsibility for
a child cannot honestly be handed over — only neglected.

## What it rules out

- Anything that treats the parent as the person who pays, drives and feeds while
  the important part happens elsewhere.
- Anything that positions us as the expert and the parent as the recipient. The
  parent decides. We supply the options, the reasons and the company.
- "Because the method says so." Every practice carries the real reason. See the
  first-principles rule in `../CLAUDE.md`.

## What it commits us to

Being genuinely useful, at a standard that justifies a parent's time. A parent
who believes this is the most important work of their life will not tolerate
filler, and should not have to.

See also: `03-isolation.md` (why doing it alone fails), `04-excellence.md` (what
we are pointed at).
