When It's Hard

The rest of the app is for a good day. This is for the other hours.

Nobody gets the calm version every time. Pick what's happening and you'll get the next minute first — then what to work backwards through, then how the traditions honestly differ. No single right answer, because there isn't one.

How to use these

  • Read only the top of the page in the moment. The rest will keep until everyone is calm.
  • The "work backwards" section is the one that changes things. Most of what helps happened hours earlier — sleep, food, how long they'd been holding it together, how the house felt.
  • The traditions genuinely disagree. Take the one that fits your child and your household, and ignore the rest without guilt.
