Welcome — and well done for being here. Whether your children are in school or learning at home, you belong.

Most of us raise our children the way we were raised, doing what everyone else does, without stopping to ask if there's a better way. Conscious parenting is simply choosing, on purpose, to be the best parent you can be — and getting a little better every day. The tools are all right here, so there's no excuse: just pick one small thing and begin.
