# Alpha School — Deep Research

_Compiled 2026-07-30. Source of truth for the app's Alpha School content (`assets/methods-data.js`, id `future-of-education`). Web-checked; where a claim is the school's own and not independently verified, it is flagged._

## What it is, in one line
A private, in-person school (not online) where children do the **core academics in about two hours a day** on adaptive apps, then spend the rest of the day on life skills and passion projects. Marketed under the banner **"2 Hour Learning."**

## Origin
- Founded **2014, Austin, Texas** by **MacKenzie Price** (psychology background, Stanford) and her husband Andrew Price, after their two daughters said school was boring.
- Now a fast-spreading network: Austin, Miami, New York, San Francisco, Brownsville, and more; new campuses announced across the US (Boston area, Oklahoma reported opening ~2026). Tuition is high — widely reported around **$40,000/year** at flagship campuses.

## How children are taught
- **Two-hour morning block** on adaptive, mastery-based apps. The "AI" is adaptive-learning software (IXL, Khan Academy, and Alpha/Trilogy's own engineers' tools) rather than a chatbot/LLM.
- **Mastery gate:** a child only advances once they've truly mastered a skill (targeting a high success band, ~70–95%), not on a fixed calendar.
- **One-to-one pacing:** every child works at their own level, so no one waits for the class average or gets left behind.
- **Camera-based engagement tracking** is used to monitor focus during the app work (a point critics flag on privacy grounds).
- **Afternoons:** ~24 real-world life-skills workshops in 8-week arcs (public speaking, entrepreneurship, financial literacy, teamwork, coding, outdoors), each building to a live challenge.
- **"Test2Pass":** workshops culminate in a live, real-stakes demonstration — do the thing (juggle, solve the cube, run a food truck, give the talk), not describe it.

## How the adults ("guides") are recruited — the distinctive bit
- Adults are called **guides, not teachers.** The software delivers the lessons; the guide's job is motivation, emotional support, life-skills coaching, and relationship-building.
- **Minimum salary ~$100,000** — deliberately high to attract strong people.
- **Only ~1/3 of guides have formal teaching credentials.** Reported guides include a Fulbright scholar, a licensed therapist / former counselor, a 30-year education veteran, a 17-year classroom teacher, Teach For America alumni.
- MacKenzie Price frames this as *transforming* the teacher role, not deleting it: once AI handles instruction, the adult is freed to know the child and light the fire.
- **Home translation:** with your own child, you are already that guide — spark, unblock, cheer; let the app do the content.

## The claims — and the honest caveat
- The school reports children **learn ~2–3x faster**, score in the **top 1–2% nationally**, and (its strongest, most human claim) **90%+ say they love school.**
- 2024–25 internal data: depending on grade, **67–90%** met math growth targets, **65–100%** met English targets.
- **Independent verification is limited.** Education researchers (e.g. NEPC, various journalists) note the biggest claims come mostly from the school itself, on a selective, high-tuition population, and are not yet confirmed by long independent studies. Frame Alpha as an exciting, watch-this-space experiment, not a settled proven answer.
