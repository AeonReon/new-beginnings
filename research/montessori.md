# Montessori — Deep Research

_Compiled 2026-07-30. Source of truth for the app's Montessori content (`assets/methods-data.js`, id `montessori`). Cross-checked against `research/01-methodologies.md` §1 and `research/03-resources-catalogue.md` §3._

## What it is, in one line
A method built by close scientific observation of children, where a **prepared environment** of real, self-correcting materials lets a child choose their own work and teach themselves — the adult prepares and observes rather than instructs.

## Origin
- **Maria Montessori (1870–1952)** — one of Italy's first female physicians. Came to education through work with children in psychiatric clinics and then poor districts of Rome.
- **First Casa dei Bambini ("Children's House") opened January 1907** in the San Lorenzo tenement district of Rome. The method spread worldwide within a decade.
- The 3–6 classroom is the **"Primary"** (or Children's House). Ours targets the 4-year-old inside that band.

## How children are taught
- **Prepared environment:** low open shelves, a small number of complete, beautiful materials each on its own tray, everything child-sized and with a home to return to.
- **Freedom within limits:** the child chooses their own work and repeats it as long as they wish.
- **The three-hour work cycle:** a long uninterrupted block; deepest concentration is observed to arrive well into it (~90 min), so interruption is avoided.
- **Practical life first:** pouring, sweeping, buttoning, food prep — real work that builds coordination, independence and focus, treated as the heart of the method, not a warm-up.
- **Self-correcting materials:** the material reveals the error (a cylinder only fits one hole), so the child self-corrects without an adult saying "wrong."
- **Sensorial + early academics through the hand:** sandpaper letters, number rods, the movable alphabet — concrete before abstract.
- **The absorbent mind & grace and courtesy:** under six the child absorbs the environment wholesale; manners are taught as explicit, named, practised skills.

## How the teachers ("guides") are trained — the distinctive bit
- Montessori adults are called **guides** or **directresses**, not teachers — the job is to prepare the environment, give precise short presentations, then step back and observe.
- Classic training is an **intensive diploma**, historically through **AMI (Association Montessori Internationale)** — the body **Maria Montessori founded herself in 1929** to protect the method — or **AMS (American Montessori Society)**. Training is heavy on hands-on mastery of every material and on observation.
- **Honest caveat:** "Montessori" is **not a trademark / protected term.** Any nursery or product can use the name, so quality varies enormously. Advice to parents: visit in person and check for trained guides, the real materials, and the protected long work cycle.

## Bringing it home (what's in the app's "Bring it home" section)
- **Nearly free:** one low shelf, a few real jobs, one protected quiet block. Charity-shop jugs and trays work fine.
- **Kit:** low open shelf; small real jugs/trays/jars; a learning tower/stool to reach the counter; sandpaper letters + number rods when interest shows.
- **Screens:** kept out of the early years on purpose. Gentlest exception if wanted: **Montessori Preschool by Edoki Academy** (edokiacademy.com) — well-regarded, follows the real sequence.
- **Free resources:** The Montessori Notebook (Simone Davies, themontessorinotebook.com); AMI essays (montessori-ami.org).
- **Books (already in the app's reading list):** Montessori's *The Absorbent Mind*, *The Discovery of the Child*; modern on-ramps — Simone Davies *The Montessori Toddler*, Tim Seldin *How to Raise an Amazing Child the Montessori Way*.

## Honest note / where it's debated
- Purist Primary rooms restrict fantasy/fairy tales before ~6 (reality-based first); many home families relax this.
- The method's strength (independence, concentration, practical competence) is widely observed; rigorous long-term comparative studies are fewer and mixed, partly because "Montessori" implementations vary so much.
