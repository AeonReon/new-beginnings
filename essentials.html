Essential Items

The short list of things that genuinely change a stage — and the reason each one works. Most of what you get sold makes no difference at all. A handful of things make an enormous one, and nobody tells you which is which until afterwards.

How this list is built

Everything here earns its place by changing what a normal day feels like, not by being nice to own. Each item says what it is, why it works, and what to look for — so you can buy it anywhere, second-hand where that makes sense, or make it yourself.

A ⭐ marks the few in each stage that make the biggest difference. There are no affiliate links and nothing here is paid for or sponsored. At the bottom is the other half of the advice: the things you can safely skip.
