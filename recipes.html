Why cook it together

The fix for fussy eating isn't a lecture, it's a countertop. When a child mashes the banana, rolls the ball, or arranges their own toppings, three things happen at once: they eat what they made, they learn where food comes from, and their taste quietly trains toward the real thing. Every recipe gives the child a job of their own — and says the real reason it beats the shop version.
